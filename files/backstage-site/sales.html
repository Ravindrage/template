Questions people ask before hiring me.

How soon could you start? +
Usually two to three working days after we agree the scope. If your situation is urgent, say so in your first message and I'll tell you honestly whether I can meet the deadline.
Is the price really fixed? +
For a defined project, yes — the number in the written scope is the number you pay. If you ask for something outside that scope mid-project, I quote it separately before doing the work. No surprise line items.
I already have a site and it's a mess. Can you work with that? +
Most of my work is exactly this — stepping into someone else's code and fixing or extending it without breaking what already works. The free audit is a good place to start.
I'm not technical. Will I be able to follow what's going on? +
You don't need to be. Describe the business problem and I'll handle the translation. Progress updates are written in plain language, not jargon.
What happens if something breaks after launch? +
Bugs in work I delivered get fixed free for a year. Beyond that, I offer a monthly maintenance arrangement if you'd rather someone else watched over it.
Will you sign an NDA? +
Yes, happily, before any details or code change hands. I work under NDA with agency clients as standard.
